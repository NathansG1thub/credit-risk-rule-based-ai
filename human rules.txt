R1. 
IF history IS past_delay
THEN decline
BECAUSE a documented history of late repayment is the most direct evidence in the file of future repayment difficulty.

R2. 
IF checking IS ONE OF [below_0, 0_to_200, none] AND employment IS unemployed
THEN decline
BECAUSE with no employment income and no visible account buffer at this bank, there is nothing to absorb a shock.

R3. 
IF instalment_rate IS at least 4 AND savings IS ONE OF [below_100, none_unknown]
THEN decline
BECAUSE the highest instalment burden leaves almost no disposable headroom, and with little or no recorded savings there is no buffer to absorb a shock — repayment capacity is exhausted before any adverse event even occurs.

R4. 
IF history IS critical_other_credits AND employment IS unemployed AND amount IS greater than 5000
THEN decline
BECAUSE with no employment income, existing credit commitments elsewhere, and a large new exposure, there is no credible repayment capacity — even a healthy account balance does not offset the absence of income.

R5. 
IF other_debtors IS guarantor AND employment IS ONE OF [lt_1y, 1_to_4y, 4_to_7y, gte_7y]
THEN approve
BECAUSE a third party guarantees repayment, sharing the default risk, and the applicant has some employment income.

R6. 
IF history IS critical_other_credits AND employment IS ONE OF [4_to_7y, gte_7y] AND amount IS at most 5000
THEN approve
BECAUSE long-established employment (4+ years) is accepted as evidence of repayment capacity in place of a local track record, deliberately overriding the 'critical account' connotation of the category on the strength of stable tenure — but only for modest exposures; larger loans still demand a local record.

R7. 
IF history IS ONE OF [all_paid_this_bank, paid_duly] AND checking IS ONE OF [0_to_200, above_200] AND employment IS ONE OF [lt_1y, 1_to_4y, 4_to_7y, gte_7y] AND amount IS at most 1000
THEN approve
BECAUSE a clean record with a visible buffer justifies a small exposure even on short employment tenure.

R8. 
IF history IS ONE OF [all_paid_this_bank, paid_duly] AND checking IS ONE OF [0_to_200, above_200] AND employment IS ONE OF [4_to_7y, gte_7y] AND amount IS greater than 1000
THEN approve
BECAUSE for larger exposures, a clean record and buffer are accepted only with long-established employment — size demands stability.

R9. 
IF history IS no_credits AND employment IS ONE OF [1_to_4y, 4_to_7y, gte_7y] AND amount IS at most 5000
THEN approve
BECAUSE with no repayment evidence on file, approval is limited to modest exposures backed by several years of stable employment.
DEFAULT. OTHERWISE decline
BECAUSE under the 5:1 cost asymmetry, declining an uncertain applicant is the cheaper error.
